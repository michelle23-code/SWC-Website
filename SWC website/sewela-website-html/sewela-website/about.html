About us

Proudly Black-owned. Focused on your success.

SEWELA Consultants was started to make business registration and tax compliance easy for South African entrepreneurs.

Too many good businesses are held back by paperwork, missed deadlines and SARS penalties. We take care of the admin — from registering your company with CIPC to filing your tax returns — so you can focus on your customers.

As a 100% Black-owned business, we are committed to supporting township, small and growing businesses with honest, affordable service.
